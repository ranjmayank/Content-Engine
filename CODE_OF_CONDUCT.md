# Code of conduct

This project welcomes everyone who wants to make content work better — beginners included.

- Be kind and specific. Critique work, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Don't post other people's private information, or anyone's secrets or keys.
- Keep promotion out of issues and pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on [github.com/ranjmayank](https://github.com/ranjmayank) or via LinkedIn ([ranjanmayank-mr](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ranjanmayank-mr/)).
