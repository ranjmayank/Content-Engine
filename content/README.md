# Content

| Folder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `blog/` | Deep posts (drafts and published copies) — `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` |
| `decode/` | Decode posts |
| `linkedin/` | LinkedIn posts and carousel copy |
| `instagram/` | Reel scripts, captions, quote-card lines |
| `topic-lists/` | Daily topic lists (JSON + markdown + email + page) |

Keeping published copies here gives the skills your back catalogue to link to and avoid repeating.
